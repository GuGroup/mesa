# Nature Communications 2025 Diction Calibration

This reference captures lightweight diction preferences observed in a small Nature Communications article sample. Treat it as style calibration, not a formal corpus law.

## Connector Preferences

- Use contrast connectors sparingly and only when the logical turn is real.
- Use additive connectors for genuine progression, not sentence padding.
- Use causal connectors only when the previous sentence directly supports the inference.
- Prefer concise transitions over ornamental phrasing.

## Claim Strength

- Reserve `significantly` for statistically supported claims.
- Use `notably`, `substantially`, or direct quantified statements only when the data justify them.
- Keep hedging in interpretation sentences, not in measured result sentences.
- Avoid achievement verbs such as `outperform`, `demonstrate`, or `enable` unless the comparison and evidence are explicit.

## Editing Checklist

- Does each connector describe a real relation?
- Is each strong verb backed by data?
- Are limitations and uncertainty placed near the claims they constrain?
- Does the sentence stay shorter when a connector is removed?
