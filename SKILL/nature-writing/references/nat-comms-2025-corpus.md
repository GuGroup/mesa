# Nature Communications 2025 Corpus Notes

This reference summarizes lightweight structural observations from a small Nature Communications sample. Use it as calibration, not as a rigid template.

## Observed Patterns

- Strong abstracts make the problem, gap, approach, result, and implication explicit.
- Introductions move from field-scale motivation to a precise unresolved gap.
- Method papers explain why the system or benchmark was chosen.
- Results sections build an evidence ladder rather than listing experiments chronologically.
- Discussion sections connect contribution, limitation, and future use.

## Writing Moves

- Make the system choice explicit when the scientific question depends on it.
- Use quantified results only when the evidence is available.
- Keep title claims bounded; avoid conclusions that are broader than the paper.
- Use contrast and causality connectors only when the logic requires them.

## Editing Checklist

- Is the gap signposted clearly?
- Is the contribution sentence tied to a specific evidence base?
- Are novelty claims scoped?
- Are limitations near the claims they limit?
