# Source-Language to English Fragment

Use this fragment when supplied notes or drafts need conversion into publication-quality English rather than simple sentence polishing.

## Rules

- Translate scientific intent, not source-language sentence order.
- Rebuild paragraphs around English claim-evidence flow.
- Keep novelty, mechanism, and performance claims bounded by supplied evidence.
- Add `AUTHOR_INPUT_NEEDED` when a comparator, metric, sample size, or mechanism is missing.
- Avoid unsupported intensifiers such as `significant`, `first`, or `innovative` unless the evidence and scope are explicit.

## Common Repairs

| Source problem | English repair |
|---|---|
| Overloaded noun phrase | Split into a clear clause or sentence. |
| Missing comparator | Add the comparator or weaken the result claim. |
| Novelty without scope | Define the field, task, or benchmark scope. |
| Method description before motivation | State the problem and design rationale first. |
