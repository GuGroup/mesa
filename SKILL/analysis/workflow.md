data expected by the selected analysis skill.

- Use `construct-energy-diagram` and `evaluate-energy-diagram` for compatible
  energy profiles.

  `charge-flow-analyzer` for compatible charge data.

Report units, conditions, reference states, and material limitations. Label
electronic energies, free energies, descriptors, and kinetic barriers
accurately. A hydrogen adsorption descriptor alone supports a bounded claim
about hydrogen adsorption.
