# Write the Results and Discussion

Read the available results, calculation summaries, figures, and notes. Organize
the prose around the scientific question and the findings the artifacts show.
Report numerical results with units and relevant conditions. Refer to figure and
table numbers where those items are included.

Distinguish the reported results from their interpretation. State material
uncertainties, failed calculations, or missing data where they limit a
conclusion. Write the section directly in the manuscript or as a separate
Markdown section.
