# Story Spine

Ni modification of compact MoS2 edge models improves simple HER descriptors, but descriptor improvement does not survive the broader alkaline HER tests performed here. Explicit H2O* to H*+OH* endpoint repairs, isolated Ni motif screening, and Ni2 interface refinement show that the best Ni-containing model is mechanistically more plausible than earlier Ni motifs, yet it remains less favorable than pristine MoS2 for the key water-dissociation product benchmark and shows strong OH retention.
