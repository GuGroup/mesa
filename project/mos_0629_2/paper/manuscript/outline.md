# Manuscript Outline

## Title

Ni-modified MoS2 improves hydrogen descriptors but does not pass alkaline HER endpoint tests in present calculations

## One-sentence argument

In Ni-doped MoS2 edge models, we show that Ni improves simple H* and H~2~O descriptors but fails to demonstrate enhanced alkaline HER over pristine MoS2 once H~2~O* dissociation, OH retention, and H-removal endpoints are included.

## Section Plan

1. Abstract: compact summary of the descriptor-positive but mechanism-negative result.
2. Introduction: why alkaline HER requires more than H* binding and why Ni-doped MoS2 is a useful test case.
3. Results: S1 descriptor benefit, S1R compact-proxy failure, S2 isolated-motif failure, S6 Ni2 partial rescue, and S6R OH/H-removal repair.
4. Discussion: interpretation of descriptor fragility, active-site identity, Ni2 limitations, and manuscript boundaries.
5. Methods: UMA endpoint workflow, AdsorbDiff placement, product endpoint gates, descriptor formulas, and VASP prepare-only rule.
6. Conclusion: bounded negative conclusion for the full enhanced alkaline HER claim.
